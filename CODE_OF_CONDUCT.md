# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in Note2Quiz welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while staying clear and accountable.
- Give actionable feedback.
- Respect differing experience levels and backgrounds.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination
- Insults, personal attacks, or sustained disruption
- Publishing private information without permission
- Any conduct that would reasonably make contributors feel unsafe

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or restrict participation when behavior harms the community.

## Attribution

This code of conduct is inspired by common open-source community standards and adapted for Note2Quiz.
